## Video — Mixkit

The hero background is the Mixkit clip "Diamond ring up close", currently
hotlinked from `assets.mixkit.co` at 720p so the project runs with nothing to
download.

License: https://mixkit.co/license/

**Before shipping this for real, self-host the clip.** Mixkit expects you to
download their file rather than link their CDN, and a hotlink can disappear
without notice. Download it, drop it in `public/media/hero.mp4`, then change
`heroVideo.sources` in `src/data/media.js`:

```js
sources: [{ src: '/media/hero.mp4', type: 'video/mp4' }],
```

`.gitignore` already keeps `public/media/*.mp4` out of the repository, so the
binary will not bloat your history.

## What was deliberately left out

Some search results showed real brand marks, logos or packaging. Those were not
used, because this is a fictional jewellery house and pairing it with a real
brand's imagery would imply a relationship that does not exist.

## Swapping any of it out

Every photograph is referenced once, in `src/data/media.js`. Change a `base`
URL there and it updates everywhere that photo appears. A product with no photo
mapped in `PRODUCT_PHOTOS` falls back to the generated artwork on its own, so
you can remove entries without breaking a page.
